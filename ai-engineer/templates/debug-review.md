Scrutiny for `ai/DIAGNOSIS.md`:

- Run the reproduction yourself. It must fail as described; a diagnosis
  whose reproduction you cannot reproduce is one objection, not a detail.
- The cause must explain every observed symptom, not just the headline
  one — name any observation it leaves unexplained.
- The ruled-out list must cover the alternatives a skeptic would
  actually raise, each killed by a checked fact, not by assertion.
- The fix sketch must be the smallest correct fix; flag anything in it
  that exceeds the diagnosed cause.
